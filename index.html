OUR STORY

Less noise, more flavour.

Osteria Velluto began as eight tables and one stove in a Brera courtyard. Today it is still a place for long dinners: seasonal produce from small farms in Lombardy and Piedmont, pasta rolled by hand every afternoon, and a room that feels like velvet the moment you sit down.

THE CHEF

Made slowly, with love

“A great plate of pasta is three things: patience, good butter and someone you love across the table.”

Chef Lorenzo Bellini trained in Bologna and Lyon before opening Velluto. His cooking is rooted in his grandmother’s Emilian kitchen, sharpened by years in fine dining.

Every pasta is rolled in-house, every sauce reduced for hours, and the menu changes with the market four times a year.

Lorenzo Bellini
